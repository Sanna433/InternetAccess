About the Project

Mapping digital rights and internet access across the globe.

What is this?

Internet Accessibility Worldwide is a student data-visualisation project that combines a 3-D interactive globe with a shutdown timeline to make the state of global internet freedom tangible and explorable.

Click on any country on the globe to see its internet penetration rate. Switch to the Shutdowns view for a year-by-year record of government-ordered internet cuts since 2009.

Glossary

Internet access: refers to the ability of people in a country to connect and use the internet. It is the amount of people who have used the Internet from any location in the country within the last 3 months. Internet access can be linked to digital TV, mobile phone, computer, etc., or any connection to the World Wide Web.

Internet shutdowns: are intentional disruptions of internet or mobile connectivity, where access is fully or partially blocked in a specific area, region, or country, usually by authorities.

Understanding the Metrics

Indexes:

Beyond simple internet access rates, we provide three key socio-economic indicators to better understand the environment of each country:

HDI (Human Development Index): A summary measure of average achievement in key dimensions of human development: a long and healthy life, being knowledgeable, and having a decent standard of living. Scale ranges from 0-1. Higher number equals better level of human development

MPI (Multidimensional Poverty Index): Captures the number and intensity of deprivations that people experience in health, education, and living standards. Scale ranges from 0-1. A higher score equals higher levels of multidimensional poverty and deprivation in a region or country.

Gini Index: A measure of statistical dispersion intended to represent the income inequality or wealth inequality within a nation. Scale ranges from 0-1. A higher score indicates greater inequality.


Risk categories:

We grouped risk of shutdown into categories based on annual shutdown frequency into low, medium, and severe risk categories based on established numerical thresholds.

Low risk: 0 shutdowns per year

Medium risk: 1-9 shutdowns per year

Severe risk: 10+ shutdowns per year

Technology

The 3D globe is rendered with Globe.GL (WebGL). Boarders are drawn de facto according to Natural Earth's disputed boundaries policy. The shutdown map uses Leaflet.js with GeoJSON country boundaries. Data is loaded from static JSON files. No backend required.
